Issue: WiFi not working

Symptoms:
- WiFi not connecting
- Internet not working
- Network issue
- Slow internet

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Turn OFF airplane mode
2. Restart your WiFi router and modem
3. Forget the WiFi network and reconnect
4. Check if other devices can connect
5. Update or reinstall network drivers
6. If still not working, contact IT support
